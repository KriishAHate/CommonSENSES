## Key things to remember

- Mount the box at **10 feet** to the bottom of the box
- Solar panel must face **true south** — 194 degrees (14 degrees offset from magnetic south)
- Verify data is uploading to [Particle Console](https://console.particle.io) before leaving the site
- Bring **2 spare boxes** in case of any failures on site
