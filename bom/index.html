    Buying in bulk

    Many of the small hardware items (screws, nuts, washers, O-rings) come in packs of 100. If you're building multiple boxes, the per-unit cost drops significantly.

    Solar panel selection

    The 5W panel is sufficient for deployments in locations with plenty of sunlight. The 10W panel is recommended for locations with limited sun exposure or harsh winters — such as Boston.
